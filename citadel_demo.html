01 / 12 The Problem
🔥
Fragmented Risk Signals
Fraud, credit, and liquidity risks live in separate systems. No single view across domains. Compliance officers waste hours correlating spreadsheets before filing a SAR.
⏱️
Regulatory Filing Delays
FinCEN mandates SAR filing within 30 days. Basel III LCR breaches require supervisory notification within 2 business days. Manual workflows miss these windows.
🕳️
No Audit Traceability
BCBS 239 Principle 2 requires full data lineage and traceability for every risk finding. Most banks have no immutable action log linking signals to outcomes.
📚
Regulation Overload
Basel III, FATF R.12/16/19, FinCEN BSA/SAR, RBI IRAC 2023 — compliance officers can't recall which rule applies to which pattern without expert lookup.
The Gap: Banks need a single AI copilot that detects signals, cites the regulation, lets the compliance officer take action with one click, and writes the regulatory narrative automatically — all inside Snowflake.

Signal: CREDIT watch-list → RWA exposure dashboard

Recommendation 12 — PEP identification and enhanced due diligence
